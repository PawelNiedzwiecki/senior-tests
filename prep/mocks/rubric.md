# Scoring rubric

Score each dimension 1–4 immediately after the timer, **before** reading any
debrief. Be harsh; the point is to find the gap while you can still close it.

| | 1 — Below | 2 — Approaching | 3 — Senior | 4 — Strong senior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Framing** | Started coding immediately | Asked one question | Restated, clarified, stated assumptions | Also proposed and agreed a signature first |
| **Correctness** | Doesn't work | Happy path only | Handles the stated edge cases | Found an edge case the brief didn't state |
| **Type quality** | `any` under pressure | Types compile, little leverage | Types prevent misuse | Types make the wrong call unrepresentable |
| **Narration** | Long silences | Narrated after the fact | Continuous, followable | Interviewer could have written it from your commentary |
| **Trade-offs** | None named | Named when asked | Volunteered with costs | Also named what you'd do differently in production |
| **Edge cases** | Interviewer found them | Found some when prompted | Listed them unprompted | Tested or guarded them |
| **Extensibility** | Follow-up required a rewrite | Awkward but possible | Follow-up dropped in cleanly | You had already left the seam |
| **Recovery** | Defended a wrong approach | Took hints slowly | Took hints, adjusted cleanly | Caught your own bug before the hint |

## Reading your score

- **Any 1**: that dimension is your single highest-value practice target. Fix it
  before adding new material.
- **Mostly 2s**: you know the material and are under-communicating. Re-run the
  same mock tomorrow and change nothing except talking more. The score usually
  moves a full point.
- **Mostly 3s**: you are in range. Spend remaining time on breadth (drills you
  skipped) rather than repeating mocks.
- **Any 4**: note what produced it and try to reproduce it deliberately.

## The two dimensions that decide close calls

**Narration** and **trade-offs**. Candidates are rarely rejected for a missing
edge case; they are regularly rejected for being hard to follow, or for making
choices without appearing to know they were choices.

If you only have time to fix one thing, fix narration.
